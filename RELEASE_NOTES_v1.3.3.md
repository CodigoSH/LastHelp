**A team's archive you can filter by several things at once, order, and read on a phone**

### Added
- In the team's archive, several statuses and several moderation states can be ticked at once in the Filters menu and applied together with one button, each one with a chip of its own that comes off by itself. The address carries them, so a view can be sent, and an address from before still works.
- The archive can be ordered: newest first, oldest first (what has waited longest) or most voted.
- The archive says how many reports the list holds under everything chosen, and announces it to screen readers.
- Choosing a repository is a button of its own beside the filters, for a team with many: it says which one is chosen, and has a box to find one by name. The groups of the Filters menu are spread across columns so every one is in sight, and each scrolls on its own when it is long.
- No team reply, the question a team asks all the time, is a button that is always in sight.

### Changed
- Ideas that shipped and bugs that were fixed are one status, Completed, in the archive's filter too: choosing it finds both and counts both, instead of listing the word twice.
- The type, All, Bugs and Ideas, is a choice in sight on a repository's page and on the roadmap, one press, and no longer something to find inside a menu.
- On a phone the menus of filters and order open as a sheet from the bottom, over the page, instead of a floating menu that could run off the screen.

### Fixed
- With No team reply on, the archive said All, with the count of everything, while the list was of what is still open. The tab that is on and the count now say the side the rows are of.
