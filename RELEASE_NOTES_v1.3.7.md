### Changed
- Notifications have one page for the whole team, under Your account in the menu, and no longer live in the profile. It holds the list of devices and, for the administrator, the channel of the server. An owner or a collaborator sees the devices; an account with no role is not offered it.
- A repository created while the server has a channel starts on it. The repositories that already exist are not moved, and choosing the server channel in one of them is still its own decision.

### Added
- When a repository of your scope tells its team by push and this browser is not on the list of devices, the overview says so under "Needs your attention" and links to the Notifications page.
