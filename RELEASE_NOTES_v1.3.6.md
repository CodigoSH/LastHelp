### Changed
- On the overview, a repository's card shows its published bugs and ideas, each with its icon and its number, instead of its published reports and its votes. A vote is a sign of interest and not a size of work, and the bulb meant an idea everywhere else on the panel. The name of each one is its tooltip.
- The reports tile of the overview does the same: bugs, then ideas, each as an icon and its number, together at the end of the line.
- Notifications about one repository share a tag, so they stack as one entry per repository, the latest on top, and each new one still alerts. They carry the small status-bar mark of the app.

### Fixed
- A browser that was forgotten while it still holds its subscription and its permission is listed again the next time the panel opens on it. To switch a device off for good, turn push off on that device itself.
- The devices of an account are pushed to in parallel instead of one after another.
